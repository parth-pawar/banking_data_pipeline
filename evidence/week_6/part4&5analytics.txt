## Joins

The banking tables are joined through their foreign-key relationships:

transactions.account_id joins with account.account_id.
account.customer_id joins with customer.customer_id.
account.branch_id joins with branch.branch_id.

This creates a combined dataset containing transaction, account, customer, and branch information. 
The joins are performed in this order because the transactions table does not directly contain customer_id or branch_id; 
those relationships are reached through the account table.

The joined dataset is then used for customer, branch, and account-type analysis
