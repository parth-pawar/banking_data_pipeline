SQLite stores the dates as strings, and the source contains multiple formats (yyyy-MM-dd and M/d/yyyy).
We parse either format and convert the column into a Spark date.

Why here: Doing it here once during loading means every downstream DataFrame gets a consistent date type without 
repeating the conversion in every transformation or analysis.
